About

Kylie Keegan

I'm a sports photographer, currently working with the Parrish High School hockey team.

I’m 26 years old and I grew up in Davie, Florida. At 14, I got my first DSLR camera and fell in love with photography. In high school, I was a part of the Media Crew. I took pictures for the school’s athletic teams and immediately knew I wanted to specialize in sports photography. I have since upgraded my equipment to the Sony a6100 and worked closely with the Ellenton Ice's 'Black Grizzlies' for two seasons.
